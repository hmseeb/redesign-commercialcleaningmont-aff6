What we do

Complete commercial cleaning, handled by one team

From nightly janitorial routes to restorative floor work, every service below is performed by trained staff using commercial-grade equipment and documented checklists.

Office & Janitorial Cleaning

Recurring nightly, weekly or custom-frequency service — trash removal, dusting, surface sanitizing, break rooms, vacuuming and detail work that keeps your workplace presentable.

Floor & Carpet Care

Hot-water carpet extraction, hard-surface scrubbing, stripping, waxing and buffing. Restorative programs that extend the life of your flooring instead of just covering wear.

Disinfection & Sanitizing

Targeted high-touch disinfection for door hardware, desks, switches, shared equipment and common areas — available as an add-on or a standalone response service.

Window & Glass Cleaning

Streak-free interior glass, entry doors, partitions and storefront windows — because the first thing a client notices is the glass they walk through.

Restroom Care & Restocking

Full fixture sanitizing, floor disinfection, odor control and consumable restocking. The single area your visitors judge hardest, kept consistently right.

Post-Construction Cleanup

Rough, final and touch-up cleans for remodels and new builds — dust removal, fixture detailing and debris clearing so the space is ready for handover day.

How it works

Four steps from first call to a cleaner building

  1. 01

    Walkthrough

    We tour your facility, note square footage, surfaces, traffic patterns and any compliance requirements.

  2. 02

    Custom Scope

    You get a written, line-item scope of work and a flat recurring price — no vague bundles, no surprise add-ons.

  3. 03

    Onboarding

    We assign a dedicated crew, set access and alarm procedures, and run a supervised first clean against the checklist.

  4. 04

    Ongoing Quality

    Scheduled inspections, a direct line to your account manager, and quick corrections whenever something needs attention.

Why Commercial Cleaning Monterey

Consistency is the whole product

Anyone can clean a building once. The value is in the two-hundredth visit looking exactly like the first. Our programs are built around trained, accountable crews and written standards — so quality does not depend on who happens to be on shift.

  • Trained, vetted crews

    Background-checked staff, uniformed and trained on your specific scope before their first solo visit.

  • Documented checklists

    Every task is written down and verified, so nothing quietly drops off the routine over time.

  • Flexible scheduling

    Nights, early mornings, weekends or day-porter coverage — we work around your operating hours, not ours.

  • Responsive communication

    A real point of contact who answers, and same-week corrections when a request comes in.

  • Commercial-grade equipment

    HEPA vacuums, extractors and auto-scrubbers — the right machine for the surface, not a one-size cart.

  • Locally owned

    Based on the Monterey Peninsula, accountable to the businesses we share a community with.
